About the course

Heavily wooded course--bring your control discs and your patience! Many challenging gaps to hit along with lots of elevation changes. A winding creek creates some interesting landing zones on a few holes as well. As far as wooded courses go, many holes are on the longer side, including five par 4's and one par 5. Very well maintained and well played, overall a great course. Course is closed for the month of November and Fridays.
