About the course

The Boulders DGC is one of two course at the North Cove Disc Golf facility. Like its name suggest. The course features incredible rock features and stunning views of the Catawba River. Open and wooded holes, lots of elevation change, and sights you don't see on almost any course including Grandfather Mountain in the background. Boulders is the longer of the two course and designed for advanced/tournament level players.
